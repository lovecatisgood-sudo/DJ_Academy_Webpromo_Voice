# DJAY Bot SaaS Platform — Final Vision Bundle v3.0

**Platform name:** DJAY Bot SaaS Platform  
**Product family:** FlowBot · AI Chatbot · Voice Agent  
**Public catalog:** exactly six subscription plans  
**Purpose:** define the final multi-tenant B2B SaaS for automating business chat and voice sales conversations.

## 1. Scope lock

This bundle is exclusively for the DJAY Bot SaaS Platform. It covers:

- deterministic rule-based chat automation;
- AI-powered text sales conversations;
- AI-powered realtime voice sales conversations;
- web and approved social messaging channels;
- shared contacts, leads, conversations, human handover, analytics, usage and SaaS billing;
- the controlled migration of the existing single-tenant FlowBot V1 and voice/text agent into the final SaaS.

It does **not** cover:

- POS or cashier systems;
- restaurant/cafe operations;
- Creative Club, child, parent, class, attendance or package-management systems;
- inventory, table ordering, employee payroll or merchant payment collection;
- a general-purpose CRM unrelated to conversations generated by these three products.

Appointment functionality means capturing appointment requests and customer time preferences. It is not a full appointment-management/POS system.

## 2. Locked public plan catalog

| Product | Tier | Customer-facing scope |
|---|---|---|
| FlowBot | Basic | Essential deterministic web chatbot automation with no AI |
| FlowBot | Premium | Advanced deterministic web chatbot automation with no AI |
| AI Chatbot | Basic | AI text chatbot on web chat only |
| AI Chatbot | Premium | AI text chatbot on web chat, LINE, WhatsApp and Facebook Messenger |
| Voice Agent | Basic | First-Generation Voice Engine |
| Voice Agent | Advanced | Second-Generation Voice Engine, the smartest voice tier |

There are exactly six public subscription plans. A tenant may subscribe to one plan from one, two or all three product families. It may not hold Basic and Premium/Advanced for the same product at the same time.

Bundles, trials, setup services, overage and negotiated limits do not create additional public plans.

## 3. Internal voice-engine mapping

Customer-facing screens, invoices, emails, widgets, errors and marketing must use only:

- **First-Generation Voice Engine**
- **Second-Generation Voice Engine**

The current internal defaults are:

- Voice Agent Basic → capability profile `voice_gen1` → `gemini-3.1-flash-live-preview` through the Google provider adapter;
- Voice Agent Advanced → capability profile `voice_gen2` → `gpt-realtime-2.1` through the OpenAI provider adapter.

Provider/model identifiers are confidential internal routing configuration. They may be viewed or changed only in the internal Platform Master Dashboard by authorized platform roles, never by Tenant Master Admin or Tenant Admin. They may be changed to an equivalent approved model without renaming the plan. This commercial confidentiality does not override legally required subprocessor or privacy disclosure.

## 4. Product statement

> DJAY Bot SaaS Platform is one workspace where businesses can deploy a predictable FlowBot, an AI text salesperson and an AI voice salesperson—individually or together—to answer customers, discover interest and pain points, reach a CTA, capture leads and request appointments.

## 5. Product boundaries

- FlowBot never calls an LLM.
- AI Chatbot is text only.
- Voice Agent handles realtime speech.
- AI Chatbot and Voice Agent share a governed Sales Conversation Core.
- FlowBot can transfer a web conversation to AI or a human only when the destination product/feature is entitled.
- Shared contacts, leads, inbox, knowledge, analytics and billing support the three products; they are not separate products.
- Autonomous self-learning is outside V1. Future improvement is evaluated, versioned and human-approved.

## 8. Delivery interpretation

This is the final SaaS vision, not an instruction to build every plan in one unreviewed change. Codex must first audit the current FlowBot V1 repository, preserve reusable behavior, and implement phase-scoped increments with migrations, tests and rollback.

## 9. Definition of product success

The platform succeeds when merchants can:

1. register a verified Tenant Master Admin on the DJAY Bot SaaS site and receive an isolated workspace;
2. launch one of the six plans without engineering support;
3. reliably receive and review customer conversations;
4. capture customer interest, pain points and contact details;
5. move qualified customers toward a CTA or appointment request;
6. understand usage, overage and outcomes;
7. upgrade between tiers without losing configuration or conversation history.
