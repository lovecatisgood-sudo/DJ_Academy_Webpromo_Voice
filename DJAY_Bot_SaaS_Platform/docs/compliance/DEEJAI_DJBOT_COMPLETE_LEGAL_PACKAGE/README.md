## Package purpose

This package provides the complete draft framework for FlowBot Basic, FlowBot Premium, AI Text Bot, Voice Bot, LINE add-on, merchant processing, Deejai marketing, partner marketing, model development, privacy governance and launch approval.

The package deliberately distinguishes:

- **Current enforceable facts**, including Neon/AWS US processing, configurable transcript retention, disabled voice recording, seven-day export access and no time-based contact purge.
- **Target policy**, including 24-month contact retention, paid-term access, a 30-day read-only/reactivation window, production purge, and backup expiry within 30 additional days.
- **Dormant features**, including partner sharing, AI, Voice and LINE, which cannot activate until product-specific approvals and technical gates pass.

The package does not describe Training Conversation Data or Model Development Data as anonymous. Direct identifier removal is a required control, while the data remains protected as personal data unless non-identifiability is demonstrated.

## Adopted operational decisions

- Merchant DPA is accepted electronically during registration.
- FlowBot Basic first year: THB 2,499 tax-inclusive; renewal: THB 4,999 tax-inclusive annually.
- Basic Deejai marketing, partner marketing and model development require separate unticked choices.

- Support target: 09:00-18:00 ICT, Monday-Friday excluding Thai public holidays; target response next business day, not an SLA.

## Use restriction

These drafts must not be mounted, shown to users or treated as legal approval until:

1. External Thai counsel approves exact versions and issues a signed approval reference.
2. An authorised director acknowledges the approval.
3. Applicable technical controls pass the Launch Readiness Certificate.
4. English and any Thai publication language are reconciled and approved.
5. Current provider contracts, data regions and operational mailboxes are verified.

No blank legal or commercial fields are left in the drafts. Where a provider, partner, dataset or feature is not yet approved, the document states that it is inactive or disabled rather than inventing an identity or commitment.
