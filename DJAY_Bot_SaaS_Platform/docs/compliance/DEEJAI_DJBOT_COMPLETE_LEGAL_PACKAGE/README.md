## Package purpose

This package provides the complete B2B draft framework for Flow Bot Starter/Advanced, AI Text Bot Starter/Advanced, AI Voice Bot Starter/Advanced, the approved Flow/Text trial terms, merchant processing, Deejai marketing, model development, privacy governance and launch approval. Social and messaging channels are excluded from the initial release.

The package deliberately distinguishes:

- **Current enforceable facts**, including Neon/AWS US processing, configurable transcript retention, disabled voice recording, seven-day export access and no time-based contact purge.
- **Target policy**, including 24-month contact retention, paid-term access, a 30-day read-only/reactivation window, production purge, and backup expiry within 30 additional days.
- **Dormant features**, including partner sharing and production AI/Voice provider processing, which cannot activate until product-specific approvals and technical gates pass.
- **Excluded features**, including LINE, Meta and every other social or messaging channel in the initial release.

The package does not describe Training Conversation Data or Model Development Data as anonymous. Direct identifier removal is a required control, while the data remains protected as personal data unless non-identifiability is demonstrated.

## Adopted operational decisions

- Merchant DPA is accepted electronically during registration.
- All six annual first-term and renewal prices are defined in the Master Terms and family schedules. DEEJAI LAB CO., LTD. is not VAT registered, so no VAT is charged and each displayed THB amount is the total contract price while that status continues.
- Flow Bot Starter and AI Text Bot Starter have the approved 30-day website-only trials; AI Voice Bot has no trial. No trial automatically converts or charges.
- Deejai marketing and model development require separate unticked choices for every tier. Partner marketing is disabled until a named partner, agreement, schedule and consent version are approved.
- Current database location is disclosed as Neon/AWS `us-east-2` until an Asian deployment is applied and independently verified.
- Customer-facing transcript retention is default 365 days and configurable from 30 to 730 days. Voice audio recording is disabled with zero retention.
- Target backup expiry is 30 days after production purge.
- Billing and tax records use a conservative seven-year target, subject to final Thai counsel confirmation.
- Working contact channels adopted for the drafts are `support@djai.academy`, `privacy@djai.academy`, `legal@djai.academy` and `security@djai.academy`; these mailboxes must be provisioned before activation.
- Support target: 09:00-18:00 ICT, Monday-Friday excluding Thai public holidays; target response next business day, not an SLA.
