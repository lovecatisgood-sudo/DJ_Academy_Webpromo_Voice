## 1. Purpose and roles

This Addendum applies where an end customer separately chooses a Deejai purpose while using Basic. Merchant controls enquiry/lead/Merchant-marketing purposes. Deejai controls Deejai marketing, partner disclosure and model development. Each is independently responsible for its purpose, notice, basis, security, retention, rights and complaints.

## 2. Consent interface

Merchant must display Deejai controls exactly as approved. Each purpose is separate, optional, unticked and understandable. Refusal must not block the Merchant enquiry. Merchant must not select, infer, bundle, conceal or improperly reward consent.

## 3. Data separation

Processor data stays in the Merchant dataset. On opt-in, only necessary data may be copied to a logically separated Deejai-controller dataset with consent evidence and source deployment.

## 4. Deejai/partner marketing

Deejai uses selected contact details only under its Marketing Consent and maintains suppression. No partner receives data unless separately consented, named, contracted and suppression-enabled. Merchant may not conceal optionality.

## 5. Model development

Governed by Model Notice/Consent. Direct identifiers are removed before dataset admission. Data is treated as personal data unless identification is no longer reasonably possible.

## 6. Rights, incidents and termination

Merchant handles Merchant-purpose requests; Deejai handles its purposes; parties redirect/cooperate. Each protects its data and notifies the other of relevant incidents. Subscription termination stops new collection, but existing Deejai-controller data remains governed by its consent/retention.
