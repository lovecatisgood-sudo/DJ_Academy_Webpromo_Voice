## 1. Default

Standard plans are not designed to intentionally collect children or sensitive data. Family/regulated businesses may use low-risk administrative enquiries unless separately approved.

## 2. Children

Child-directed bots require age/guardian flow, notice, lawful consent, minimisation and counsel approval. Preferred standard flow asks a parent/guardian to submit contact. Known child conversations are excluded from model development.

## 3. Sensitive data

No intentional request for racial/ethnic origin, politics, religion/philosophy, sexual behaviour, criminal records, health/disability, union, genetic, biometric or other sensitive categories in standard plans.

## 4. Regulated sectors

Healthcare, legal, finance, insurance, recruitment and education may use FAQs, hours, service descriptions, appointments and human contact. No diagnosis, prescription, eligibility decision, rejection or final professional advice.

## 5. Credentials/payment

Passwords, PINs, OTPs, private keys, full card numbers, CVV and online-banking credentials are prohibited. Stripe-hosted checkout is used for subscription payment.

## 6. Unexpected data

Restrict access, notify Merchant where appropriate and delete/restrict under incident/legal-hold process.
