## 1. Required file

Legal entity, service, data, role, countries, subprocessors, security materials, DPA, breach terms, deletion/retention, support access, government requests, continuity and termination export.

## 2. AI questions

Provider training use, prompt/output retention, zero-retention options, human access, abuse monitoring, regions, routing and deletion.

## 3. Voice questions

Number ownership, call countries, recording/transcription storage, consent tooling, caller-ID, suppression, lawful interception, emergency limits and deletion.

## 4. Partner questions

Purpose, channels, consent, onward sharing, suppression, child/sensitive prohibition, retention, security, complaints and controller contact.

## 5. Rating/review

Critical/high/medium/low based on sensitivity, scale, country, access, replacement and impact. Critical/high require privacy, security and management approval. Review onboarding, annually for critical/high, after incidents and before material change.

## 6. Current gaps

Email provider, DNS/registrar/TLS role, Neon backup/deletion, Google contracting entity, AI/Voice providers and marketing partners remain unapproved.
