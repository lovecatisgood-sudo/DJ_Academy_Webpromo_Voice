## 1. Scope

AI Text Bot produces Thai and English website responses grounded in Merchant-provided business information and approved knowledge sources. The Merchant selects Customer Support, Sales Associate or Appointment Booking behavior. Output is limited to 200 visible grapheme characters per customer reply.

## 3. Term, renewal, cancellation and refunds

Paid subscriptions run for one year and renew automatically for successive one-year terms at the accepted renewal amount unless cancelled before renewal. Cancellation is effective at paid-term end. Fees are non-refundable except where required by law, for a verified duplicate or incorrect charge, or for the unused period if Deejai terminates without Merchant breach. An approved order controls any more favorable term.

## 4. Starter trial

The Merchant may configure and test AI Text Bot before providing payment details. When the Merchant presses Deploy Bot, Stripe validates and saves the card without a DJBOT charge. If the card has not previously received a Text trial, the Merchant may receive AI Text Bot Starter settings for 30 fixed days from successful deployment provisioning, website only, with 500 AI-generated replies. The trial does not renew, convert or charge automatically. At 100 replies remaining, Deejai warns the account owner in the platform and by email. New AI replies stop at exhaustion or expiry and the Merchant is invited to subscribe.

Eligibility is one Text trial per Stripe card fingerprint, stored only as a keyed hash. No company, domain, telephone, IP or device verification is required. No additional warning recipient is used without a separately approved notification decision.

## 5. Merchant duties and output limits

The Merchant must provide accurate sources, test Thai and English behavior, monitor output, correct known errors, configure human escalation and disclose that the customer is interacting with AI. Output may be incomplete, outdated, biased or wrong. The Service must not make final decisions on employment, credit, insurance, education admission, healthcare, housing or legal rights, provide diagnosis or prescriptions, act as emergency response, impersonate deceptively or collect credentials.

## 6. Data and AI processing

Prompts, outputs, conversation context and approved sources are processed for Merchant service under the DPA. They are not used for Deejai model development unless a separate, freely given consent and the Model Development Notice apply. The production AI provider, contracting entity, processing regions, retention, training controls and transfer safeguards must be listed in the approved Subprocessor Schedule before activation.

## 7. Provider status

The codebase contains an internal Text runtime and an OpenAI Responses adapter. xAI Grok and Google Gemini are permitted provider candidates but do not yet have approved Text adapters in this workspace. The current local launch configuration does not supply a complete live AI gateway/provider route, and no unmocked production acceptance has been recorded. AI Text Bot therefore remains disabled for sale and must fail closed when the selected gateway is unavailable.
