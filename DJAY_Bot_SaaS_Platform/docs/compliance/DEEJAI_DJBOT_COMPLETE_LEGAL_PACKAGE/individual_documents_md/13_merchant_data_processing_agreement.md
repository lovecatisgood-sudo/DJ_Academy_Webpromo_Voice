## 1. Parties and scope

This DPA forms part of the Master Terms. Merchant is controller and DEEJAI LAB CO., LTD. processor for Personal Data processed on Merchant instructions. It is accepted electronically during registration.

## 2. Definitions

PDPA meanings apply. "Merchant Personal Data" means end-customer/staff data processed for Merchant purposes. "Subprocessor" means a processor appointed by Deejai.

## 3. Instructions

Deejai processes Merchant Personal Data only to provide, secure, support and improve the subscribed Merchant service, follow documented instructions and comply with law. Terms, schedules, settings and support requests are instructions. An unlawful instruction may be suspended and disclosed to Merchant unless prohibited.

Deejai marketing, partner marketing and model development are separate controller activities and not authorised by this DPA.

## 4. Merchant obligations

Merchant warrants authority and lawful basis; must provide notices, minimise data, avoid prohibited categories, maintain accuracy, respond to Data Subjects and not instruct unlawful processing.

## 5. Personnel/confidentiality

Authorised personnel are subject to confidentiality and role-appropriate access.

## 6. Security

Deejai maintains reasonable measures in Schedule 2, considering nature/risk/state of art/cost. Security commitments are a program, not a guarantee against all incidents.

## 7. Subprocessors

Merchant gives general authorisation for Schedule 3 providers. Deejai imposes appropriate obligations and remains responsible as required. Material new subprocessors receive reasonable notice; Merchant may object on substantiated protection grounds. If no solution, the affected Service may be terminated without future renewal liability.

## 8. Transfers

Merchant Personal Data is currently processed through Neon/AWS in Ohio, USA. Deejai documents safeguards and updates the notice only after verified migration.

## 9. Rights assistance

Merchant decides/responds to requests for Merchant purposes. Deejai assists through available export/erasure jobs, subject to authentication, rate limits, legal holds and residuals. Direct requesters may be referred to Merchant.

## 10. Erasure

Current erasure removes/replaces identifying content and may retain non-identifying shells for integrity, audit or legal hold. It is not removal of every row. Backups/provider copies expire under implemented controls.

## 11. Breach

Deejai notifies Merchant without undue delay after confirming a breach affecting Merchant data and provides available information. Merchant remains responsible for controller notifications; Deejai cooperates and may make notices required of it.

## 12. Records/audits

Deejai maintains required records and provides reasonable compliance information. Audits normally use policies, certifications, reports, questionnaires and remote review. On-site audit is limited to legal requirement or material unresolved evidence and is subject to confidentiality, scope, security and cost.

## 13. DPIA assistance

Reasonable information is provided. Material custom work may be charged at agreed rates.

## 14. Return/deletion

Available exports may be obtained during the applicable window. After termination, data is returned/erased under implemented controls except law, claims, security evidence or legal hold. Current and target states appear in the Retention Standard.

## 15. Liability, precedence and term

Master Terms liability applies to the lawful extent. This DPA prevails for processing conflicts. It continues while Deejai processes Merchant data; protective clauses survive.
