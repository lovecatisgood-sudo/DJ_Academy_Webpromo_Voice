## 1. Scope

AI Voice Bot provides Thai and English automated voice conversations through an approved website voice deployment. Telephone capability is excluded unless a separately approved carrier, region, price, disclosure and product entitlement are active. The validated written response is limited to 200 visible grapheme characters before speech output.

## 3. No trial

AI Voice Bot has no free trial. A demonstration, test session or internal pilot does not create a customer trial, paid entitlement or production availability promise.

## 4. Term, renewal, cancellation and refunds

Paid subscriptions run for one year and renew automatically for successive one-year terms at the accepted renewal amount unless cancelled before renewal. Cancellation is effective at paid-term end. Fees are non-refundable except where required by law, for a verified duplicate or incorrect charge, or for the unused period if Deejai terminates without Merchant breach.

## 5. Disclosure and recording

The Service must identify the Merchant and automated nature of the interaction and explain transcription, human alternatives and recording status before collecting customer speech. Audio recording is disabled with zero recording retention. Recording may be enabled only under a new approved legal version with consent-required mode, approved Thai and English scripts, a retention period from 1 to 365 days and a recorded legal approval reference. Transcripts remain personal data and follow the approved message-retention policy.

## 6. Merchant duties and restrictions

The Merchant must test Voice behavior, confirm material details, provide human escalation and avoid emergency response, diagnosis, prescription, final legal/financial advice, full card details, passwords, one-time passcodes, unlawful discrimination, deceptive impersonation and final high-impact decisions. Outbound or telephone use is prohibited in the initial release.

## 7. Providers and current status

The codebase contains a provider-neutral Voice WebSocket gateway and adapters for Google Live and OpenAI Realtime. xAI Grok Voice is a permitted provider candidate but is not yet implemented or approved in this workspace. The runtime requires an explicit enable flag, session-authority endpoints, media endpoints and provider credentials; the current local launch configuration does not provide that complete route. No live provider acceptance is recorded. Voice therefore remains disabled for sale and fails closed when media configuration is incomplete.
