## 1. Basic is data-supported

Merchant uses the chat for an enquiry. Deejai operates the platform and may offer separate choices for Deejai marketing, approved partner marketing and model development.

## 2. Core use

The customer may chat without optional Deejai consent. Merchant receives submitted contact/message data. Deejai processes it to deliver and secure the Merchant service.

## 3. Deejai marketing

Only if separately selected, Deejai may use name/email/telephone for DJBOT, DJAI Academy and related digital-service marketing through selected channels. Withdrawal is available.

## 4. Partner marketing

Only if separately selected and a Partner Schedule names recipients. No partner is active, so this remains disabled.

## 5. Model development

Only if separately selected, a controlled conversation copy may be used. Direct identifiers are removed, free text screened, and known child/sensitive conversations excluded. Data is not called anonymous.

## 6. Retention and rights

Transcript period is 30-3,650 days, default 365. Deejai marketing contact is retained until withdrawal or 24 months after last engagement, whichever first; suppression evidence five years. Model data follows the Model Notice. Merchant handles enquiry rights; Deejai handles its choices at https://djai.academy/privacy/preferences or privacy@djai.academy.
