## 1. Dormant status

Voice Bot is inactive until telephony, speech-to-text, text-to-speech and AI providers, call countries, scripts, consent, suppression and retention are approved.

## 2. Permitted use

When activated it may answer inbound calls, make authorised outbound calls, qualify leads, provide general information, schedule appointments and transfer to a person. Merchant controls call purpose/list.

## 3. Disclosure

At call start, identify Merchant, automated system, recording/transcription status, human-transfer and marketing opt-out.

## 4. Recording

Recording is disabled and forced to zero retention. It may be enabled only with an approved period of 1-365 days, required consent mode, approved Thai/English scripts and legal approval reference. Transcription may still require disclosure.

## 5. Outbound calls

Merchant must have a lawful list/basis, observe hours, identify the business and honour suppression. No cold-call abuse, repeated harassment, spoofing or calling suppressed persons.

## 6. Restrictions

Not an emergency service. No diagnosis, prescription, final legal/financial advice, full card details, passwords/OTP or final high-impact decisions.

## 7. Accuracy and data

Confirm material details because speech/AI can be wrong. Audio/transcripts are processed under the DPA. Model development requires separate consent and direct-identifier removal. Child/sensitive conversations are excluded unless approved.

## 8. Providers

No telephony or speech provider is approved; feature remains disabled.
