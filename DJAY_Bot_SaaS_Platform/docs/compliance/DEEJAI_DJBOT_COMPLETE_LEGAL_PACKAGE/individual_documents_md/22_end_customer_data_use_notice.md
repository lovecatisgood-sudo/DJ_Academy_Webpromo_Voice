## 1. Merchant service

The customer uses a Merchant's Flow Bot, AI Text Bot or AI Voice Bot for an enquiry or other Merchant service. Deejai operates the platform and may offer separate choices for Deejai marketing and model development. Partner sharing and social-channel delivery are disabled for the initial release.

## 2. Core use

The customer may chat without optional Deejai consent. Merchant receives submitted contact/message data. Deejai processes it to deliver and secure the Merchant service.

## 3. Deejai marketing

Only if separately selected, Deejai may use name/email/telephone for DJBOT, DJAI Academy and related digital-service marketing through selected channels. Withdrawal is available.

## 4. Partner marketing

No partner receives end-customer data in the initial release. Any future partner use requires a new notice naming the recipient and a separate optional consent.

## 5. Model development

Only if separately selected, a controlled conversation copy may be used. Direct identifiers are removed, free text screened, and known child/sensitive conversations excluded. Data is not called anonymous.

## 6. Retention and rights

Transcript period is 30-730 days, default 365. Deejai marketing contact is retained until withdrawal or 24 months after last engagement, whichever first; suppression evidence five years. Model data follows the Model Notice. Merchant handles enquiry rights; Deejai handles its choices at https://djai.academy/privacy/preferences or privacy@djai.academy.
