## 1. Purpose

With separate consent, Deejai may use Training Conversation Data to develop, test, evaluate and improve language models, retrieval, safety filters, intent classification and conversation quality.

## 2. Data

Conversation text, bot response, flow step, broad language, error category and quality label may be used. Direct identifiers such as name, email and telephone are removed before dataset admission. Identity-bearing free text may be quarantined/excluded.

## 3. Not anonymous

Removing direct identifiers may not prevent contextual re-identification. Deejai treats the data as personal data unless identification is no longer reasonably possible.

## 4. Exclusions

Known children, sensitive data, passwords, OTPs, full cards, identity documents, medical diagnoses, criminal-history and other high-risk content are excluded. Suspected material is quarantined.

## 5. Human review

Authorised confidential reviewers may inspect limited samples for quality, safety and de-identification. Access is logged; re-identification and other use are prohibited.

## 6. Providers/transfers

No external AI provider is approved. Before any transfer, the Subprocessor Schedule and Notice must identify provider, region, retention and provider-training setting.

## 7. Retention

Consent/eligibility evidence: five years after withdrawal/last reliance. Quarantine: up to 30 days. Approved datasets: up to 24 months. Dataset/model provenance: seven years. A completed model may not contain a directly retrievable conversation, but removing one record's influence may be technically impossible.

## 8. Withdrawal

Use https://djai.academy/privacy/preferences or privacy@djai.academy. Withdrawal stops future collection/use. Active eligible records are removed where reasonably possible. It does not undo prior lawful processing or necessarily remove effects from a completed model.

## 9. Security/contact

Logical separation, access control, registry, minimisation and monitoring apply. Direct identifiers are prohibited in approved training datasets. Contact privacy@djai.academy.
