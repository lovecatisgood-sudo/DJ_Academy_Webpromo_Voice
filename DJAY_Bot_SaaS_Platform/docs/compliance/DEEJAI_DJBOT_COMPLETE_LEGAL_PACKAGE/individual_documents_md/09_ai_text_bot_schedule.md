## 1. Dormant status

AI Text Bot is inactive until the Manifest names the approved AI provider, region, retention, model behaviour and counsel approval reference.

## 2. Service

It may generate responses using Merchant instructions, context and approved knowledge sources; classify enquiries; retrieve information; recommend next steps; or hand off to a person. It must not silently make a high-impact decision.

## 3. Merchant duties

The Merchant must provide accurate sources, test and monitor output, correct known errors, configure human escalation and disclose AI interaction. AI must not substitute for qualified professional judgment.

## 4. Accuracy

Output is probabilistic and may be incomplete, outdated, biased or wrong. Prices, legal/medical/financial information, eligibility and safety matters require independent verification.

## 5. Prohibited uses

No final decisions on employment, credit, insurance, education admission, healthcare, housing or legal rights; no diagnosis/prescription; no emergency response; no unlawful discrimination, deceptive impersonation or credential collection.

## 6. Data

Prompts, outputs, context and sources are processed for Merchant delivery under the DPA. Separate model-development use requires the Model Notice and Consent. Direct identifiers are removed before dataset admission; sensitive and child data are excluded unless separately approved.

## 7. Providers/transfers

No AI provider is approved. The feature must remain disabled until the Subprocessor Schedule identifies provider, entity, data, region, retention, training settings and safeguards.

## 8. Human escalation and sources

A human contact must be available for material complaints/transactions. The Merchant warrants rights to uploaded sources and must remove unnecessary personal/confidential/unlawful content.

## 9. Suspension

Deejai may suspend AI functions where output/use creates material safety, legal, privacy, security or reputational risk.
