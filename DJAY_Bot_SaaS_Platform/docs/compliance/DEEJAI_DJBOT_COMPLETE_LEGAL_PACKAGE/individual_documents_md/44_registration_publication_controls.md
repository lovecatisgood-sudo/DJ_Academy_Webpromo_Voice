## 2. Publication checklist

Merchant identity/contact displayed; Merchant and Deejai notices linked; optional controls separate/unticked; core chat works without optional consent; no passwords/OTP/full cards/ID docs; no intentional sensitive/child data; marketing separate; content/prices/contact accurate; human help available.

## 3. Plan controls

Every Starter and Advanced tier uses the same privacy boundary: Deejai marketing and model-development choices are separate and optional, partner choice is disabled, and the Merchant has the approved model-development disable control. A higher tier never expands Deejai's data-use permission.
