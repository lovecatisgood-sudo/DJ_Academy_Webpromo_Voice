## 1. Central suppression

Maintain suppression by purpose/channel. Deejai withdrawal stops Deejai marketing but not automatically Merchant marketing.

## 2. Minimal record

Hashed/normalised identifier, purpose, channel, withdrawal date, source and evidence reference. Retain five years after last attempted use unless law/claim requires longer.

## 3. Timing

Electronic unsubscribe/preference applies immediately to future selection. Manual requests entered without undue delay. Campaigns refresh suppression before send.

## 4. Partner propagation

Send signed suppression instruction to each relevant partner and record confirmation. Partner must stop contact and report direct withdrawals.

## 5. Re-consent

Only a new affirmative action using current text can override suppression. Old list import cannot.

## 6. Monitoring

Quarterly tests of campaign exclusion, partner propagation, channel coverage and re-import. Failure is a privacy incident.
