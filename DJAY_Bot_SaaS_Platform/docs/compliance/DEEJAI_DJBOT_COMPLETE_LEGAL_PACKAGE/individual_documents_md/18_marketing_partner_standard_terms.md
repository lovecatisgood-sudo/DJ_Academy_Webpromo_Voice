## 1. Application and roles

Applies when a partner accepts an electronic partner order incorporating these terms. No partner is active merely because this document exists. Deejai and Partner are independent controllers for the approved marketing purpose.

## 2. Preconditions

Deejai must hold auditable separate consent covering the partner/category, data, purpose and channel. Partner must provide legal identity, privacy notice, rights/security contacts, countries and retention.

## 3. Restrictions

Partner may use data only for approved category/channels/territory/period and must not sell, profile for unrelated purposes, onward-disclose, contact suppressed persons, infer sensitive traits, target children, make high-impact decisions or over-retain. Campaign subprocessors require appropriate terms.

## 4. Marketing and suppression

Every message identifies Partner/purpose and provides simple opt-out. Partner observes lawful hours/frequency. Deejai sends withdrawals; Partner applies them without undue delay and confirms. Partner also reports direct withdrawals.

## 5. Security/incidents/audit

Reasonable access, encryption, logging, incident and deletion controls; confidentiality; prompt incident notice to security@djai.academy; cooperation and evidence including campaign samples, suppression and deletion confirmation. Deejai may suspend transfers.

## 6. Termination/liability/law

On termination or consent expiry, stop and delete/suppress except required evidence. Each party is responsible for its unlawful processing. Thai law and Bangkok courts apply unless the order lawfully states otherwise.
