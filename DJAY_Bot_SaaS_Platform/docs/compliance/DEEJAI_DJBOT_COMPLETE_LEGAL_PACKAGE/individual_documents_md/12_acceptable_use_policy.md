## 1. Rule

Use only for lawful, transparent and proportionate business communication. Merchant is responsible for flows, prompts, sources, lists, channels and instructions.

## 2. Prohibited uses

- Fraud, deception, theft, unlawful surveillance or illegal trade.
- Harassment, threats, exploitation or unlawful discrimination.
- Malware, phishing or harmful code.
- Password, PIN, OTP, full card or authentication-secret collection.
- Deceptive impersonation.
- Spam or marketing without lawful basis and opt-out.
- Scraping, probing, overloading, bypassing limits or interfering with security.
- Intentional sensitive-data collection without written approval.
- Child-directed collection without guardian process.
- Emergency dispatch claims.
- Final high-impact decisions.
- Unreviewed personalised medical diagnosis/prescription, final legal or investment advice.
- IP/privacy/confidentiality infringement.
- Unauthorised resale, white-label or sublicensing.
- Circumventing or falsifying consent.

## 3. Restricted sectors

Healthcare, finance, insurance, legal, education, recruitment and child-related businesses may use general information, administrative enquiries, appointments and human follow-up. Higher-risk use requires separate approval.

## 4. Enforcement/reporting

Deejai may investigate, disable flows/features, suspend, preserve evidence or terminate. Urgent action may be without notice. Report abuse to legal@djai.academy or security@djai.academy.
