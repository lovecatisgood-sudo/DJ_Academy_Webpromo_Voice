Controllers/processors implement appropriate security and records. Qualifying breaches may require PDPC notice within 72 hours and high-risk data-subject notice. Electronic contracts can be effective where identity, intention, integrity and accessibility are reliably evidenced. VAT-related records are generally at least five years and may be required up to seven; package adopts seven.
