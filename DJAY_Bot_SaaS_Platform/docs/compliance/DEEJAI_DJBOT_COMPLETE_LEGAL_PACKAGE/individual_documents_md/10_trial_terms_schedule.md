## 2. No automatic charge or conversion

The Merchant may configure and test the AI Text Bot before providing a card. When the Merchant presses Deploy Bot, Stripe validates and saves the card using a SetupIntent without creating a DJBOT charge. The issuing bank or card network may show a temporary verification entry; Deejai does not promise or collect an exact USD 1 debit. A card collected for the AI Text trial does not authorize an automatic charge, subscription or conversion. No trial renews automatically. A paid subscription begins only after the Merchant receives and affirmatively accepts a separate order showing the amount due, renewal, taxes, allowances and applicable legal terms.

## 3. Trial matching and availability

Flow eligibility is limited to one trial for each normalized verified account email. Text eligibility is limited to one trial for each payment card, enforced using a keyed hash of the Stripe card fingerprint after successful card setup. Deejai does not require a company registration number, business-domain verification, business telephone verification, IP identity or device identity for trial eligibility. IP/device signals are not a sole rejection reason. Deejai may decline or stop a trial for demonstrated fraud, unlawful use or material breach, without converting it into a paid service.

## 4. Data after trial

The ordinary DPA and currently enforced retention/erasure controls apply. The Merchant may submit an authorised export or erasure request subject to law and legal hold. Trial-claim records are retained as security and contract evidence for as long as the trial programme operates and then deleted under the approved erasure process, subject to legal hold.
