## 1. Scope

FlowBot Basic is a rule-based website chatbot and lead-capture service. It excludes AI-generated answers, voice calls, knowledge-file uploads, Meta channels and included social channels.

## 2. Subscription

- THB 2,499 tax-inclusive first year.
- THB 4,999 tax-inclusive renewal year.
- Annual automatic renewal unless cancelled before renewal.
- One active bot, one workspace and one included administrator.
- 50,000 flow executions per billing period.
- No automatic overage billing; processing may stop at the limit.
- Target maximum of three Merchant-controlled domains/origins, subject to technical enforcement.
- No separate lead-count cap, subject to fair use and legal restrictions.

## 3. Functions

The Merchant may build rule-based flows, forms and calls to action; embed the widget on approved origins; capture names, emails, telephone numbers and free-text enquiries; view leads; and submit privacy export/erasure jobs.

A one-click general lead CSV export is not promised until released. Subject-data export exists through privacy jobs.

## 4. Local storage

The widget sets no cookies. It uses one first-party localStorage entry per deployment containing an opaque session token. Blocking storage should not prevent basic chat.

## 5. Installation and support

Self-serve. Included support does not build the Merchant flow. Optional Flow Starter Setup is from THB 3,900. Support is email-only. No SLA or service credits.

## 6. Data uses

For Merchant enquiries/leads: Merchant controller, Deejai processor. Basic may offer separate unticked choices for Deejai marketing, partner disclosure and model development. Refusal must not prevent chat. Partner sharing is disabled until a partner is approved. Direct identifiers may not enter a training dataset.

## 7. Retention

Message content defaults to 365 days and may be configured 30-3,650 days; then it becomes a non-content tombstone. Contacts/leads currently remain until erasure or closure handling. A 24-month inactivity purge applies only after implementation and notice update.

## 8. Add-ons

Additional administrator THB 99/month; workspace from THB 299/month; supported social channel THB 299/month; LINE only under its Schedule. Messenger, Instagram and WhatsApp are unavailable.

## 9. Restrictions

Not approved for emergencies, diagnosis, personalised final legal/investment advice, credentials/OTP/card collection, intentional sensitive data, child-directed collection without guardian approval or automated high-impact decisions.
