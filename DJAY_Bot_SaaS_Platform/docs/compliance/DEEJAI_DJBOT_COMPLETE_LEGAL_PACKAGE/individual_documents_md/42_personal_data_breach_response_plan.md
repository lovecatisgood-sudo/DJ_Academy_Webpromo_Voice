>
> **Release rule:** This document must not be published or relied upon as a production promise until the Launch Readiness Certificate confirms that every dependent control has been implemented, tested and approved. Current-state facts control over target policy until verification.

## 1. Reporting

Suspected loss, unauthorised access/disclosure/alteration/unavailability, model leakage or suppression failure must be reported immediately to security@djai.academy; do not wait for certainty.

## 2. Team/log

Incident lead coordinates security, privacy, engineering, operations, legal and communications. Privacy Lead assesses data risk/notification. Record facts, awareness time, containment, systems and deadlines.

## 3. Immediate actions

Preserve evidence; contain accounts/tokens/datasets/providers; identify data/persons/countries/Merchants/time; assess ongoing harm; notify affected Merchant controllers without undue delay.

## 4. Regulatory assessment

Assess whether PDPC notification is required within 72 hours after awareness, to the extent feasible, and whether high-risk notice to affected persons is required. Document delayed/non-notification reasons.

## 5. Merchant notice

Known nature, categories, approximate persons/records, consequences, measures, recommended actions and contact, with updates as facts develop.

## 6. Providers/special incidents

Invoke provider contacts and obtain findings. Suppression failure: halt campaign. Model leakage: quarantine model/dataset. Export exposure: revoke object/token. Tenant isolation failure: suspend affected access and scope cross-tenant records.

## 7. Closure

Close after containment, decisions, remediation, lessons, owners and verification. Retain incident records seven years or longer under hold.
