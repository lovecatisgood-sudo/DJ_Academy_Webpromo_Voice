## 2. Scope and roles

This Notice covers Merchant owners/staff, billing/support contacts, visitors and end customers where Deejai is controller. For Merchant enquiry/lead data, Merchant is normally controller and Deejai processor under the DPA. Deejai is controller for account administration, billing, security, Deejai marketing, approved partner disclosure and model development.

## 3. Data collected

Merchant name; staff name/email; authentication/account/role records; billing identity/subscription; a normalized verified-email claim for Flow trial matching; a keyed hash of the Stripe card fingerprint for Text trial matching; support communications; IP, request/security and device/browser data; consent and preferences. Deejai does not receive or store the full card number.

Through Merchant bots: name, email, telephone, free-text messages, lead information, opaque session token and website deployment identifiers. Starter and Advanced are service tiers, not privacy permissions. Deejai marketing and model development require separate optional choices. Partner sharing and social-channel delivery are disabled for the initial release.

## 4. Sources

The person, Merchant, Merchant site/channel, Stripe, approved providers and security systems. A Merchant providing another person's data must have authority and give required notice.

## 5. Purposes and bases

Account/service/billing/support, enforcing the published one-trial-per-email or one-trial-per-card rules, security/fraud, law/claims and rights administration rely as applicable on contract, legal obligation and legitimate interests. IP and device data are not used as the sole reason to reject a trial.

Deejai contact marketing, partner disclosure and model development rely on separate consent in the initial release. Consent may be withdrawn for future processing.

## 6. Model development

Training Conversation Data is not described as anonymous. Direct identifiers are removed and free text screened before dataset admission. Known child and sensitive-data conversations are excluded. Data remains protected as personal data unless identification is no longer reasonably possible. See the Model Development Data Notice.

## 7. Cookies/local storage

FlowBot sets no cookies. It uses one first-party localStorage entry per deployment containing an opaque session token. Any future optional analytics will require an update and any required choice.

## 8. Recipients

Neon and its AWS infrastructure are current hosting recipients. Stripe receives Merchant card and billing data when an approved paid checkout or AI Text trial SetupIntent is used. Google Cloud is planned but its Asian production deployment is not active until verified. No transactional-email, AI or Voice provider is approved for production processing. Social providers, including LINE and Meta, are excluded from the initial release.

## 9. International processing

The current primary database is Neon on AWS `us-east-2`, Ohio, USA. Personal data therefore may be transferred outside Thailand. Provider agreements and applicable safeguards are used and transfer risk is assessed. Notice changes only after verified migration.

## 10. Retention

Chat and Voice transcript content defaults to 365 days, and the current Merchant API permits 30-730 days; after expiry, content becomes a non-content tombstone. The database can technically hold a higher value, but the customer-facing policy and API maximum is 730 days. Voice audio recording is disabled with zero recording retention unless a later approved legal version and technical policy enable it.

Privacy export downloads are available seven days. The expired encrypted result row is not yet physically purged. Contacts/leads remain until erasure or closure handling; no automatic time purge is active. Billing/tax records: seven years. Consent evidence: five years after withdrawal/last reliance unless claims/law require longer. Legal holds continue until release.

Target after verification: contacts/leads 24 months inactivity; 30-day read-only/reactivation after paid term; production purge; backups within 30 additional days. Those controls are not represented as active until verified.

## 11. Rights

Subject to law: access/copy, portability where applicable, correction, deletion, restriction, objection and consent withdrawal. Merchant customers should contact Merchant for Merchant purposes. Deejai-purpose requests: privacy@djai.academy or https://djai.academy/privacy/preferences when available.

Identity may be verified. Lawful limitations may apply. Erasure may remove identifying content while retaining non-identifying shells, audit evidence, legal-hold data and backup copies until expiry.

## 12. Marketing preferences

Every Deejai/partner marketing message provides opt-out. Withdrawal does not affect prior lawful processing. Minimal suppression evidence may be retained to avoid renewed contact.

## 13. Security and breaches

Reasonable controls include access restriction, tenant separation, transport encryption, provider security, logging and incident response. No system is completely secure. Breaches are assessed and notified under applicable duties.

## 14. Children and sensitive data

Standard Service is not designed to intentionally collect child or sensitive data. Child-related Merchants should collect guardian contact and obtain separate approval for higher-risk processing. Do not submit passwords, OTPs, full card data, identity documents or unrequested sensitive data.
