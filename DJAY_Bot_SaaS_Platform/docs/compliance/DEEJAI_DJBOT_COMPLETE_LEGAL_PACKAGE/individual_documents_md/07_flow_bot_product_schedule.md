The currently enforced Merchant-configurable maximum must control if lower than any draft policy. Flow Bot is not an emergency service and must not collect passwords, one-time passcodes, full card data or unnecessary sensitive data, or make final high-impact decisions. The Merchant must test each published journey and provide lawful notices and a human escalation route where appropriate.
