From prospect to a working customer conversation

The complete merchant journey begins with a bot and package decision, creates authoritative access, configures that purchased product, proves a live channel, then moves into daily customer operations.

Commercial authoritySix annual packages. The catalogue controls price, limits, channels, usage and availability.
Configuration authorityThe purchased product determines setup. A business goal can recommend relevant starting content, but never removes editing or review.
Launch authorityA browser button never marks launch complete. Published version, test evidence, channel verification and entitlement determine readiness.

The dependency that must never be reversed

Bot family and package are chosen before product onboarding. Business outcome is a configuration input inside the purchased product.

Package → product capability → business outcome → editable configuration → test → publish → channel verification → live operation

Example: Flow Advanced plus “Book appointments” opens an appointment-capable Flow starting point. The merchant must still edit the wording, questions, required fields, availability source, timezone, confirmation behavior, handover, failure fallback and notifications. Nothing is accepted by a forced “looks right” button.

Three independent product lifecycles

A merchant may own multiple families. Each keeps separate configuration, test, deployment, health and usage state.

Flow Bot

Predictable journeys

Rules, buttons, branches, forms, lead capture, requests, handover and deterministic execution.

  • Starter: website, 1 bot, 150 topics, 50,000 conversations
  • Advanced: up to 3 bots, 500 topics, 100,000 conversations, one social channel, advanced logic and integrations
AI Text Bot

Grounded open-text conversations

Approved knowledge, structured sales behavior, citations, actions, qualification and escalation.

  • Starter: website, 1 bot, 2,000 replies, Thai and English
  • Advanced: up to 3 bots, 10,000 replies, one social channel, routing, integrations and advanced analytics
AI Voice Bot

Website voice and telephone

Disclosure-first voice, grounding, interruption, callbacks, appointments, transcripts and outcomes.

  • Starter: web voice, 1 agent, 150 minutes, 1 concurrent session
  • Advanced: up to 3 agents, 500 minutes, telephone, transfer, 2 concurrent sessions and integrations

Cross-realm merchant-to-customer chain

No page is complete until its downstream authority and recovery behavior are clear.

Catalogue and package authority

The pricing experience must display the exact first-year charge, annual renewal, limits, allowance, channels, support, overage and current availability before checkout.

Add-ons, packs and professional services

These are separate commercial decisions and must never appear included in a base package.

Thirty-day free-trial branch

Flow Bot and AI Text Bot prospects receive two distinct choices on pricing: subscribe or check eligibility for a 30-day website trial. Voice remains subscription-only.

Trial lifecycle

Trial onboarding uses the real selected-product setup path. Trial status changes entitlement and usage, not the ability to edit and test configuration.

Rules already requested and rules still requiring approval

Tentative values and missing commercial rules stay visibly unresolved.

Every merchant-facing screen

Select a row to inspect purpose, entry and exit paths, controls, dialogs, authoritative states, failures, recovery, package rules and downstream effects.

Onboarding logic by purchased product

Shared business facts are collected once. Product-specific steps remain separate, editable, resumable and evidenced by real configuration.

How the selected business goal changes configuration

The same named goal opens different controls for each purchased bot family. It recommends an editable starting point only; it never publishes sample content or forces acceptance.

Website, social and external integration flows

A connection is not live when credentials are merely saved. Identity, entitlement, reachability, inbound and outbound behavior, fallback and health must all be proven.

Merchant bot to end-customer journeys

The public widget or channel conversation creates durable conversations, contacts, leads, requests, handovers, outcomes, notifications and usage evidence for the merchant.

Merchant workspace before and after launch

The dashboard is available while configuration is incomplete and highlights Configuration as not configured. Configuration remains a separate full-page workspace that the merchant can reopen at any time.

Complete merchant feature ledger

Every capability is tied to a product, operating surface and lifecycle. Inclusion here does not override package entitlement or a blocked release gate.

Popups, confirmations, errors and recovery
